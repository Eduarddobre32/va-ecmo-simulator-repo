Privacy Policy

VA-ECMO Trainer · Last updated: 7 July 2026
Summary: VA-ECMO Trainer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The application does not require an account, login, or registration. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

2. Data Storage and Transmission

All simulation runs locally on your device. The app does not connect to the internet to send or receive your data, and it contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits (SDKs).

3. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for medical education and a professional/adult audience. As it collects no data, it poses no data-privacy risk to any user, including minors.

4. Permissions

The application requests no sensitive Android permissions. It functions as a self-contained, offline educational tool.

5. Educational Use Only

This application is a simplified teaching model of veno-arterial ECMO physiology. It is not a medical device and must not be used for clinical decisions, diagnosis, treatment, or patient care.
